Everything you need to know about enrollments, instruments, and exams.

For Vocal and Fine Arts, children can begin as early as 4 to 5 years old. For Carnatic Violin, we recommend starting from age 6+, where hand size and finger dexterity allow comfortable holding of 1/8, 1/4, or 1/2 size violins. Adult learners are welcome at any age!
For the trial session, you don't necessarily need the instrument; we will assess pitch perception, rhythm (Taalam), and recommend the exact size and model suitable for purchase in the UK or your country.
